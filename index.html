      Home / Privacy

      Privacy policy

      Last updated November 2026.

      What we collect

      Your name, phone, email, address or postal code, details about the items to remove and any photos you send.

      Why

      To quote, schedule and complete your job, to contact you about it and to keep the records tax law requires. We never sell your information.

      Consent

      Submitting our form or contacting us means you agree to these uses. Promotional messages only go out if you separately agree, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

      Sharing

      Only with our website host, our form provider and a payment processor if you pay electronically, unless the law requires otherwise.

      Cookies, analytics and ads

      This site may use the Meta Pixel and Google Analytics to measure visits and to show our ads to people who have visited. These tools use cookies and share browsing information, such as pages viewed and whether a quote request was sent, with Meta and Google. They never receive the contents of your quote request. You can block these cookies in your browser settings, and manage ad preferences in your Facebook and Google accounts.

      Retention

      Quote requests that don't become jobs are deleted within 12 months. Job and invoice records are kept as long as tax law requires.

      Your rights

      Under Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) you can ask to see, correct or withdraw consent for the information we hold. Email damian.carson@dcremovalca.com and we'll respond within 30 days.

      Photos

      Photos you send are used only to quote your job. We ask before using any photo of your property in our marketing.
